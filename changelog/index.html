What’s new?

Release history for Hishab Ledger.

VERSION 1.0.0

A refreshed Hishab Ledger experience.

Version 1.0.0 improves the home dashboard and makes key areas easier to reach.

  • Updated dashboard with Total Payable and Total Accounts summary cards
  • Bottom navigation for Home, Add and Settings
  • Search field on the home screen for shops and people
  • Settings entry point for language, backup/export, app lock, recently deleted and app information
  • English and Bengali language selection, with translation coverage still being improved
  • Existing local ledger records, due/payment history, reminders and data export/backup workflows retained

Note: Some planned full-screen forms and complete theme/translation work are not yet included in this build.

VERSION 1.0.0

Hishab Ledger launch.

The first public release introduced the Hishab Ledger app identity and core ledger workflows.

  • Shop and person records
  • Due and payment transactions
  • Opening balances and transaction history
  • Local reminders and notifications
  • Recently Deleted and undo support
  • JSON backup and restore
  • Full and shop-wise CSV export
  • PDF statements and shareable summaries
  • PIN and biometric app lock
  • Offline-first local data storage

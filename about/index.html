About Hishab Ledger

Made to make due tracking easier.

Hishab Ledger is an independent Android app focused on one practical job: keeping personal due and payment records simple, portable and easy to review.

Simple by design

A focused interface with direct actions instead of a complicated accounting workflow.

Offline-first

Core records live locally on the device, so normal use does not require an account or internet connection.

Portable data

JSON backup/restore, full CSV export and shop-wise CSV export help you keep control of your records.

Wafy Labs

Created by Afee Muhammod Wafy.

Hishab Ledger is a Wafy Labs project, created by Afee Muhammod Wafy as an independent utility for everyday personal due tracking. Version 1.0.0 launches the Hishab Ledger identity with the existing core ledger functionality preserved: dashboard summaries, shop/person records, due and payment history, reminders, backup and restore, exports, sharing and app lock.

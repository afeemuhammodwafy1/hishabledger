Features

Simple tools. Clear records.

Hishab Ledger 1.0.0 focuses on a simple personal workflow: balances, transactions, summaries, sharing and portable backups.

S

Multiple shops

Keep separate records for different shops or people and see each current balance independently.

Σ

Total Shops & Total Due

The home dashboard gives a quick count of shops alongside the overall current due.

+

Due & Payment

Add a due when credit is given and a payment when money is received.

B

Opening balance

Enter the amount already due before using Hishab Ledger. This becomes part of the current balance.

H

Transaction history

See clear Due, Payment and Opening Balance entries with amounts, exact date/time and optional notes.

↶

Delete + Undo

Delete a transaction with confirmation, then use the Undo action in the snackbar to restore it immediately.

Σ

Today summary

Review today's added dues, payments, net change and the current due for a shop.

N

Optional notes

Attach a short note to a transaction when extra context matters.

☎

Phone actions

Save a phone number for a shop or person and choose Call or Copy from the record screen.

↗

Share statement

Share a concise balance statement with current due, total due added and total paid.

PDF

PDF statement

Export an individual record statement as a PDF file.

CSV

CSV export

Export all records as CSV or export one record's history separately.

JSON

Backup & restore

Create JSON backups and restore them later. The restore flow supports Replace, Merge or Cancel.

OFF

Offline-first

Core records are stored locally. No account or internet connection is required for normal app operation.

Built for focus.

Fast navigation, straightforward actions and no unnecessary accounting clutter.
